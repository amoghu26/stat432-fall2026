title: "When Does Local Averaging Stop Being Local?"
author: "Amogh Upadhyaya (amoghu2)"
---

In high dimensions the neighborhood needed to catch k points stretches across almost the whole range of each variable, so "nearest neighbors" aren't really near. At what point do we stop calling it local averaging and admit it's just averaging?
