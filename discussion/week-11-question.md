title: "Can PCA Before Clustering Discard the Groups?"
author: "Amogh Upadhyaya (amoghu2)"
---

PCA keeps the highest-variance directions, but clustering wants groups that are far apart, and those aren't always the same directions. So can running PCA before clustering throw away the low-variance directions that were actually separating the groups?
