title: "How Much Should Lasso Selection Flips Worry Us?"
author: "Amogh Upadhyaya (amoghu2)"
---

Lasso can zero out one of two correlated predictors and keep the other, but which one it keeps can flip with a different sample or fold split. If the variable list isn't stable, how much should we read into which variables lasso "selected"?
